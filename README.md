# Enterprise Business Operations Analytics & Executive Decision Intelligence

An enterprise analytics project that brings together sales, marketing,
customer, inventory, finance, and operations data to support executive
decision-making using Excel, Power Query, and Power BI.

## Business Objective

Build a centralized reporting solution that helps executives monitor
organizational performance, identify operational bottlenecks, and prioritize
data-supported actions across departments.

## Stakeholders

- CEO / Executive Leadership: business health, risks, and strategic priorities
- Sales: revenue, targets, products, regions, and customers
- Marketing: campaign performance, conversions, and investment
- Finance: budgets, costs, profitability, and variances
- Operations: delivery, returns, complaints, and fulfillment
- Inventory: stock availability, turnover, and supplier performance

## Project Workflow

1. Map business processes and stakeholder requirements.
2. Clean, standardize, and validate source data.
3. Integrate data from relevant business functions.
4. Define department and executive KPIs.
5. Analyze trends, variances, contributions, correlations, and root causes.
6. Build interactive dashboards and scorecards.
7. Validate calculations and document assumptions.
8. Present findings and actionable recommendations.

## Limitations

Results depend on the completeness, accuracy, and timing of source data.
Measures such as marketing ROI, customer lifetime value, inventory turnover,
and delivery performance require clearly defined inputs and calculation rules.
Document unavailable fields, assumptions, and known data-quality issues.
